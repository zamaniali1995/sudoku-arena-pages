Privacy Policy — ALI Play Sudoku Arena

Last updated: October 3, 2026

ALI Play Sudoku Arena does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No account required. You can play solo without signing in to anything.
  • No tracking. The app does not track you across other apps or websites, and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Game Center (optional). If you choose to play online races, Apple's Game Center handles matchmaking, party codes, and leaderboards under Apple's own privacy terms. We do not receive, store, or see your Game Center data.
  • On-device only. Your settings, statistics, and profile picture are stored only on your device and never leave it.

If you contact us for support, we will use your email address only to reply to you.
